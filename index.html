🏛️ FDA Official Guides — Read These

📋 FDA OFFICIAL GUIDE
Cosmetic Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP)
FDA’s standard for how cosmetics must be manufactured. Diego’s factory must meet this to ship to the US. If they’re ISO 22716 certified, they already comply. Ask Diego this question before any US distributor conversation.
Covers: documentation, facilities, equipment, personnel, raw materials, production SOPs, lab controls, internal audit, complaints + recalls. Prohibited ingredients listed (none apply to Drogam’s products). Color additives must be FDA-approved.
PDF · fda.gov · Updated 2013 · Save This
Why This Matters for Drogam
EEG/Bradley project: Luisa’s lab must follow GMP standards when developing the 4 new skincare formulas. Any client (including EEG’s end customer) will ask for GMP documentation before placing commercial orders.

US distributors: E.F. Tropics and CosmoProf will ask “is your manufacturer GMP-compliant?” before listing your products. This document tells you what that means.

Ask Diego: Is the Cali factory ISO 22716 certified? If yes — you’re already GMP-compliant for most US buyers. If no — add it to the roadmap.
